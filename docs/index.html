CLIENT DEMO · AI MARKET INTELLIGENCE

News-to-Market Impact Intelligence

A LangGraph and Streamlit application that analyzes uploaded news or public URLs, maps events to company products and raw-material exposure, and generates explainable market-impact research signals.

Launch Live Demo View Source Code

What the demo shows

News Intelligence

Upload a news file or provide a URL and extract key market drivers and event context.

Company Exposure

Connect companies, products, regions and raw-material dependencies to the news event.

Impact Signals

Generate INCREASE, DECREASE or NEUTRAL research signals with impact score and confidence.

Conversational Follow-ups

Ask company-specific questions or retrieve direct master-table values without rerunning the full report.

Guardrails & Evaluation

Apply input/output guardrails and response-quality evaluation with optional LLM-as-Judge escalation.

Traceability

Store execution logs and evaluation details for each analysis run.

High-level workflow

News / URL → Guardrails → LangGraph → Exposure Engine → Impact Report → Evaluation

Research demonstration

The application generates research-oriented directional signals. It does not guarantee future stock-price movement and is not a substitute for regulated investment advice or independent financial analysis.
